Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are site quotes free?

Use the quote form or contact RHAVI to request a free site quote. Share your area and the work required so the team can confirm the next steps for your project.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency callouts?

RHAVI is listed as open 24/7. Call or WhatsApp to explain the issue and confirm availability, service requirements and the callout arrangements.

Can complexes and body corporates request ongoing work?

Yes. Townhouse complexes, residential estates and property managers can contact RHAVI to discuss maintenance requirements, project scope and a suitable quotation.

Do you provide a workmanship guarantee?

Ask for the applicable workmanship terms for your specific project. Any scope, exclusions and agreed guarantee period should be confirmed in writing with the quotation before work begins.
